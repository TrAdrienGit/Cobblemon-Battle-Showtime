# Battle intro references

A library of videos of the battle intros of every Pokémon game, to draw new Showtime intros from. Links only: never
put game footage or assets in this repository. To study one closely, download it outside the repository and cut it
into contact sheets with ffmpeg (fps=2, tile=8x5), as done for Battle Revolution.

Each intro we build is our own drawing of the idea (shapes, rhythm, camera), never a copy of the game's art. Status:
**done** = an intro in `BattleCinematic.Intro`; **idea** = worth building.

## Next intros worth building

1. **Sun/Moon "shattered screen"**: the view breaks into shards that fly away, then the opponent's pose (our trainer
   emotes can play it).
2. **Stadium fly-over** (Stadium 2, Colosseum): a long camera flight over the field before the trainers.
3. **Gen I/II wipes**: spiral and stripes, pixel style, very short: for players who want speed.
4. **Emerald Elite Four / Champion wipes**: one striking wipe each, for a "boss" feel.
5. **Scarlet/Violet seamless**: no 2D at all, two camera moves and the battle starts.
